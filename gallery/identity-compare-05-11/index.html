How to read it without fooling yourself

  • Frame count changes the meaning. Noa's 0.527 rests on 5 faces; Roni's 0.517 on 10; Lea's 0.565 on 11. A median over more pairs is harder to hold, so 10 frames at 0.517 is a stronger result than 5 at 0.527.
  • The weakest pair is the real test. Noa's tail (0.474) is the tightest in the table. Roni's weakest pair sits at 0.400 — above noise, but visibly weaker than her median.
  • The clothing filter is part of the gate. Sets that are not clothed cannot enter the canon format at all, regardless of identity score. That is why Shira shows "no SFW set" here rather than a number.
  • Nothing here says a character is good. This measures one property: does the same face survive across frames. Art direction, motion and story are not in this table.

What I did not do

I did not rank candidates. The table is sorted by median, but a 3-frame set and a 12-frame set are not the same achievement, and a set that fails the clothing gate is not "worse" — it is simply not in this format. The sets I could not measure (Shira, the LoRA runs) are listed with the reason, not hidden.

Method

Detection YuNet, confidence ≥0.75, minimum face width 80px · Alignment 5-point similarity transform to 112×112 · Embedding ArcFace w600k_mbf, L2-normalised · Score cosine similarity, median over all pairs, reported with p10 and minimum · Clothing pre-filter skin-ratio on the torso band ≤25% · Dedup 12×12 average hash, Hamming <10 merged.
