How to read the "weakest" column

Two genuinely different characters score a median of 0.058 and never exceeded 0.221 across 120 measured pairs. So a weakest pair at 0.400 (Roni) sits far above anything a stranger produces; a weakest pair at 0.194 (Noa 22/10) is inside the range strangers occupy.

Set definitions — so these numbers can be challenged

A number without its set is not a measurement. Each figure above comes from a fixed file list:

  • Roni — 12 raw frames of one session; 10 passed detection (conf ≥ 0.75, face ≥ 80px). Two rejected before measuring.
  • Lea — the 17 frames pinned in her page's gallery array; 11 passed detection. 6 files filtered out pre-detection by a clothed-frame check.
  • Noa extra — 15 files in noa-extra-22-10, 5 pass the clothed check and detection. Noa 22/10 and Layla — 3 faces each after the same filters.
  • Shira — no frame in the indexed folders passes the clothed check, so there is nothing to measure. That is a content decision, not a technical failure.

What I would submit, and why

Roni, with the entry pack and the verification bundle. Not because it has the highest median — Lea does, at 0.565 — but because it is the only entry where every claim is reproducible by a stranger in one command, and where motion, evidence and disclosure are already assembled. Lea is stronger on paper and blocked by an owner decision, so the right move is to have her ready, not to submit her.

The two days left are better spent making one entry bulletproof than making five entries look similar. That is also the honest reason this page ranks Roni first: not quality, readiness.

Corrections on this page's history
