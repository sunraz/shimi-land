## Prompt (verbatim)
Fixed tripod camera, framing locked in place, background walls identical in every frame, only the two bodies move. Two women in identical pale sky-blue lace training tops and matching lace bottoms train in a bright studio, both fully in frame head to toe. Constant continuous movement. The woman on the left holds the other's wrist, turns her hips and steps behind the arm, then sweeps the other's legs out from under her so she drops to the mat on her back. The woman on the mat immediately bridges her hips, pulls her arm free, rolls away and comes back up to her feet in a fighting stance. Real takedown and escape mechanics, hips rotating, legs driving, feet stepping, hands gripping and releasing, sharp fast body-driven motion the whole clip, technique clear in the arms and legs. Not a dance, not slow, no pose holding, costume unchanged.
