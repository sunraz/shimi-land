## שני השוטים החדשים (רונדרו במיוחד לעריכה הזו)
### beat_punch
Two women in identical pale sky-blue lace training sets standing at fighting distance, full body in frame, fixed camera. The woman on the right fires one fast straight punch at the other's face; the other's head snaps back and she steps off the line. Explosive sudden speed, hard snapping motion, motion blur on the fist, feet driving off the floor, the fist stops short without touching. Not slow, not gentle, not a dance.

### beat_kick
Two women in identical pale sky-blue lace training sets standing at fighting distance, full body in frame, fixed camera. The woman on the left snaps one fast low round kick into the other's thigh; the other's leg buckles and she recoils back a step. Explosive speed, sharp snap and retraction, motion blur on the shin, the kick stops short without touching. Not slow, not gentle, not a dance.

## Negative (שניהם)
slow motion, gentle, graceful, romantic, dance, ballet, swaying, hug, embrace, contact, camera movement, pan, zoom, blurry, distorted, extra limbs
